Hello, my name is Spyros Bakalakos

Game Developer, Designer, Software Engineer

I'm a Software engineer with an integrated master's degree in Informatics and Computer Engineering from the University of West Attica. I recently completed my specialization in Game Design at the University of Gothenburg where I received my MSc in Game Design and Technology.

I have previously worked as a Unity developer for 3D applications, Augmented Reality applications and Virtual Reality applications on various fields including robotics and Digital Twin projects. I am also an independent game developer working with a variety of clients.

My main occupation is designing and developing innovative and engaging video games for a wide variety of platforms. My interests and skills spread accross a multidisiplinary field from programming, gameplay design, level design, animation and sound to asset creation including 3D modeling 2D sprites and visual effects.
